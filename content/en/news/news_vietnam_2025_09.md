        From September 17-20, 2025, the 23rdVietnam International Plastics & Rubber Industry Exhibition wassuccessfully held at the Saigon Exhibition & Convention Center in Ho ChiMinh City. Dongguan Jinjiu Automation Technology Co., Ltd. made a remarkableappearance at the event, showcasing its advanced automation solutions to theSoutheast Asian market.

        During the exhibition, Jinjiu Automation'score products, including Automatic Control Air Rings, Gravimetric BlendingSystems, and Film Thickness Gauges, attracted significant attention fromprofessional visitors due to their excellent performance and intelligentfeatures. The company's team engaged in in-depth exchanges with clients fromacross Southeast Asia, gaining valuable insights into local market demands thatwill inform future technology development and market expansion.

        This participation effectively enhanced theJinjiu brand's visibility in the Southeast Asian market, strengthenedrelationships with existing partners, and created new business opportunities.Moving forward, Jinjiu Automation remains committed to continuous innovation,providing global customers with high-quality, efficient automation equipmentand solutions to support the intelligent transformation and upgrading of theplastics and rubber industry.
